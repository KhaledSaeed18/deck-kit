Script

Will 5G replace Wi-Fi? No: they work together.

Wi-Fi uses free, unlicensed spectrum; 5G uses licensed spectrum that operators pay for.

Wi-Fi is set up by you, a company or a university; 5G is run by an operator.

Wi-Fi is best for capacity indoors; 5G is best for coverage on the move.

And they share the work: phones move a lot of their data to Wi-Fi when they can.

More questions

  • How much traffic is offloaded? In the US, 82 to 89% of smartphone data goes over Wi-Fi (Opensignal, 2024). That is a US figure; no reliable global one exists.
  • How do phones move between them? Passpoint and OpenRoaming join trusted Wi-Fi automatically; 3GPP Release 16 added ATSSS to steer traffic across both, and NR-U to run 5G in unlicensed bands.
