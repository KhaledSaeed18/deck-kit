Is this still true?

Script

This is Table 1.5 from Lecture 1, slide 29. It lists wireless technologies with their speed and range.

Look at three rows: Bluetooth at 1 Mbps, Wi-Fi at 54 Mbps, and WiMAX at 75 Mbps.

So: is this still true?

These are the textbook's figures; several were already overtaken by 2010. At the end of the talk we'll update the table.
