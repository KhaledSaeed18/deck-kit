Script

Back to the table we started with, updated for 2026.

Wi-Fi: from 54 Mbps to about 23 Gbps with Wi-Fi 7, and Wi-Fi 8 on the way. This is the row that changed the most.

Cellular: 2G, 2.5G and 3G have given way to 4G and 5G.

Bluetooth: now version 6.x. Its speed is still only 1 to 3 Mbps; what improved is battery life and range.

WiMAX: largely displaced by LTE/5G.

Three takeaways: (1) coverage defines the class; (2) Wi-Fi went from speed, to efficiency, to reliability; (3) Wi-Fi and cellular complement each other.

If asked

  • Will 5G replace Wi-Fi? No. Licensed vs unlicensed spectrum and operator vs owner economics make them complementary.
