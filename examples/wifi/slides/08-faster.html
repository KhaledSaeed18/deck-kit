Script

Each generation got faster by turning one of three dials.

Wider channels are more lanes on the highway. Wi-Fi channels grew from 20 MHz to 320 MHz with Wi-Fi 7. The catch: fewer wide channels fit in a band.

More bits per symbol: each signal carries more data, from 6 bits to 12 bits with Wi-Fi 7. The catch: it needs a strong, clean signal, so top speed only happens close to the router.

More antennas are parallel conveyor belts: several data streams travel at once on the same channel, up to 8. A phone usually has 2.

Acronyms

  • MHz: megahertz (channel width)
  • QAM: Quadrature Amplitude Modulation
  • MIMO: Multiple-Input Multiple-Output

If asked

  • What is QAM? The way bits are packed into each radio symbol: 64-QAM carries 6 bits, 4096-QAM carries 12.
  • Why does my phone not reach the top speed? It has 2 antennas, not 8, and it is rarely close enough for the densest signal.
More detail and sources

More questions

  • How many bits per symbol? 64-QAM carries 6 bits, 256-QAM 8, 1024-QAM 10, 4096-QAM 12. 1024 to 4096 is only 20% more.
  • How clean must the signal be? Roughly 25 dB SNR for 256-QAM, 31 dB for 1024-QAM and close to 42 dB for 4096-QAM, so 4096-QAM works only very near the AP.
  • How many streams? 802.11n up to 4; 802.11ac, ax and be up to 8. A phone with 2 antennas gets 2.

Nuances

  • MIMO streams share the same frequency at the same time; they are separated in space by the antennas and multipath, not by extra spectrum.
