Kerckhoffs' principle

A cryptosystem should be secure even if the attacker knows everything about it, except the key.

Auguste Kerckhoffs, 1883

secret design means untested design

Script

In 1883 Auguste Kerckhoffs wrote down the rule the whole field still follows. Assume Oscar knows your algorithm. Only the key is secret.

It feels backwards: surely hiding the design makes it stronger? Experience says no. A secret design is an untested design, and secrets leak or get reverse-engineered. The DVD Content Scrambling System was broken quickly once it was reverse-engineered in 1999.

Hiding the design is called security by obscurity. Publishing it is how a cipher earns trust.

If asked

  • Isn't a secret algorithm extra protection? Only until it leaks, and you lose the free analysis of every cryptographer in the world. AES was chosen in an open, public competition run by NIST from 1997 to 2000 for exactly this reason.
  • Where was it published? "La cryptographie militaire", Journal des sciences militaires, January and February 1883.
