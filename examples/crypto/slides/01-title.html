The Key Is the Only Secret

shift every letter back by three

Paar and Pelzl · Fall 2026

Script

Before I say anything, look at the line on the screen. It is my title, encrypted the way Julius Caesar is said to have done it.

Can anyone read it? Give it a few seconds.

Shift every letter back by three and W becomes T, K becomes H, H becomes E. "The key is the only secret."

That sentence is the whole of chapter 1 in one line. By the end you will know why it is true, and how a cipher with more keys than there are grains of sand can still be broken with a pencil.

If asked

  • Why shift by three? Suetonius writes that Caesar used the fourth letter of the alphabet, D, in place of A. That is a shift of three.
